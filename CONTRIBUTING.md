# Contributing to Squeeze Series

Thanks for helping make this the most complete guides collection on the internet. Here's how to contribute well.

## What we accept

- **Corrections** — wrong data, broken links, outdated numbers (with a source for the fix)
- **New data points** — verified stats with official sources
- **New guides** — see the roadmap in README; propose before writing a full guide
- **Translations** — help localize guides into other languages

## What we don't accept

- Promotional content, affiliate-heavy writing, or product pitches
- Unverified claims ("I heard that...") without sources
- AI-flavor writing (delve, tapestry, leverage, notably, "in conclusion" — we check)
- Anything that isn't a complete, self-contained guide

## Content rules (every guide)

1. **Data first** — every claim cites a source. Official docs > independent research > anecdote.
2. **Year-stamped** — data must say which year it's from (e.g. "as of 2026").
3. **Actionable** — end with what to do, not just what to know.
4. **Self-contained** — each chapter works on its own; add a table of contents.
5. **Honest** — if a number isn't verified, say so. Never invent data.

## Process

1. Fork the repo
2. Make your change (one guide per PR)
3. Self-check: banned AI words = 0, every claim sourced, data year-stamped
4. Open a PR with a short description of what changed and why

## Style

- Plain English, short sentences, varied rhythm
- Tables for comparisons (they read better than walls of text)
- Em dashes sparingly (max 2 per chapter)
- Be opinionated — readers want a point of view, not a Wikipedia entry
